KnitID: Machine-Knitted UHF RFID Antennas for Battery-Free Sensing and Interaction

WI Lab, University of Washington · 11/2025 – present

Knitting magnet wire into textiles to make small, battery-free RFID antennas that identify, localize, and sense interaction on the body.

Details
  • Designed a machine-knitted UHF RFID antenna that reaches about 1.4 m on-body read range in a 60 × 8 mm footprint; mapped the design space (wire coating, knit structure, wale count, conductive length) with far-field simulation and empirical sweeps.
  • Built an RSSI + phase sensing pipeline and a physics-constrained bidirectional-GRU localization model: 100% tag identification, about 85% interaction recognition accuracy, around 6 cm localization error.
  • Demonstrated battery-free textile interfaces for robot-arm control, exercise tracking, and a smart wardrobe.

AuthGlass: Voice Liveness Detection and Authentication on Smart Glasses

Pervasive HCI Lab, Tsinghua University · 07/2025 – 02/2026

The first open smart-glasses dataset and benchmark for telling a real wearer's voice from replay, injection, and impersonation attacks.

Details
  • Built a synchronized 16-channel glasses prototype (14 air-conduction + 2 bone-conduction mics, 96 kHz) and collected 30K+ genuine and attack utterances from 42 participants (~188 GB, open on Hugging Face).
  • AuthG-Live: a liveness detector built on sound-field time-delay and energy-ratio patterns learned from genuine speech only; >96% accuracy across 7 attack settings and robust to unseen attacks.
  • AuthG-Net: a multimodal CNN with domain-adversarial training for passphrase-independent authentication; >97% accuracy with about 1% drop on unseen phrases.
  • Benchmarked 11 baselines across 4 tasks. The inter-channel feature scheme shipped in Alipay's production voice verification stack.

BIT: Battery-free, IC-less and Wireless Smart Textile Interface and Sensing System

XD Lab (SFU) & Pervasive HCI Lab (Tsinghua) · 09/2023 – 11/2024

Smart textiles that sense touch and gestures with no battery and no chip, powered and read wirelessly through resonant coils.

Details
  • Proposed a wireless-power-transfer sensing model for textiles based on multi-resonant circuits and near-field coil coupling.
  • First hardware–algorithm co-design that recovers sensor state from impedance spectrum analysis.
  • Fabricated a VNA-based auto-solver and textile interfaces including a glove and a shirt (PCB design, soldering, auto-embroidery).
  • First inventor on a U.S. patent assigned to Florida State University.

User Verification for Smartwatch Tap-to-Pay

Machine Learning Engineer Intern, Trust & Safety · 06/2026 – 09/2026

End-to-end verification for paying with a watch: data protocol, data quality, an IMU + PPG model, and the interaction flow around it.
