Research Experience

KnitID: Machine-Knitted RFID Antennas for Battery-Free Sensing and Interaction 11/2025 – present
Graduate Research Assistant, WI Lab, University of Washington
  • Integrated magnet wire into the loop-over-loop structure of industrial machine knitting to make a UHF RFID antenna with about 1.4 m on-body read range in a 60 × 8 mm footprint; mapped the design space (wire coating, knit structure, wale count, conductive length) with far-field simulation and empirical sweeps.
  • Built a multimodal RSSI + phase sensing pipeline and a bidirectional-GRU localization model with physics-based constraints for trajectory reconstruction and real-time interaction sensing: 100% tag identification, about 85% interaction recognition accuracy, and around 6 cm localization error.
  • Co-first author; accepted as an ACM UIST 2026 poster, with the full paper submitted to ACM CHI 2027.
AuthGlass: Voice Liveness Detection and Authentication on Smart Glasses 07/2025 – 02/2026
Project lead, Pervasive HCI Lab, Tsinghua University, in collaboration with Ant Group
  • Built a synchronized 16-channel smart-glasses prototype (14 air-conduction + 2 bone-conduction microphones, 96 kHz) and collected the AuthGlass dataset: 42 participants, 30K+ genuine and attack utterances across 7 attack settings (~188 GB, released on Hugging Face).
  • Designed AuthG-Live, a liveness detector built on sound-field time-delay and energy-ratio patterns learned from genuine speech only: >96% accuracy across 7 attack settings and robust to unseen attacks.
  • Designed AuthG-Net, a multimodal CNN with domain-adversarial training for passphrase-independent authentication: >97% accuracy, about 1% drop on unseen phrases.
  • First-author paper in ACM IMWUT 2026.
BIT: Battery-Free, IC-Less and Wireless Smart Textile Interface and Sensing System 09/2023 – 11/2024
Project lead, XD Lab (Simon Fraser University) and Pervasive HCI Lab (Tsinghua University)
  • Proposed a wireless-power-transfer sensing model for textiles based on multi-resonant circuits and near-field coil coupling, with a hardware–algorithm co-design that recovers sensor state from impedance spectra.
  • Fabricated a VNA-based auto-solver and smart textile interfaces including a glove and a shirt (PCB design, soldering, auto-embroidery).
  • First-author paper at ACM CHI 2025; first inventor on a U.S. patent assigned to Florida State University.
